# Colonel Fatboy brand voice

Rules
- No hyphens or dashes of any kind, anywhere. They read as machine written.
- No greeting card filler. No corporate sign offs. Do not parrot other businesses.
- Never recite the product catalogue as a message. Products earn their place.
- Write like a pitmaster who means it, not a marketer.
